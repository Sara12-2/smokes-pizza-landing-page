## 🎯 Overview

**Smoke's** is a modern, responsive landing page designed for a pizza, burger, and loaded fries restaurant in **Kallar Syedan**. The website is built to convert visitors into customers by making ordering easy through WhatsApp and phone calls.

### Purpose
- Showcase the restaurant's menu and deals
- Provide a seamless ordering experience
- Build brand trust with a professional online presence
- Drive customer engagement through easy contact options

### Key Goals
- **Conversion-focused**: Direct WhatsApp ordering
- **Mobile-first**: Optimized for all screen sizes
- **Fast loading**: Lightweight and optimized
- **Brand consistent**: Matches Smoke's bold, smoky aesthetic

## ✨ Features

### 🏠 Hero Section
- Bold animated headline with emphasis on "UNFORGETTABLE"
- Dual CTA buttons (WhatsApp order + Menu explore)
- Quick info badges showing delivery time, freshness, and hours
- Eye-catching food visual with rotating price tag
- Seamless scrolling ticker showcasing popular categories

### 📋 Menu Section
- **Category Filters**: All, Burgers, Pizza, Pasta
- **Smooth animations** when filtering items with fade-in effect
- **Direct WhatsApp ordering** for each item with pre-filled messages
- **Size options** for pizzas with individual pricing (S, M, L, XL)
- **Keyboard accessible** filter navigation with roving tabindex
- **Card hover effects** with scale and shadow animations

### 🎯 Deals Section
Three value combo deals with direct WhatsApp ordering:

| Deal | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| 🍕 Evening Deal | Rs. 1,600 | 1 Large Pizza + 1L Drink |
| 🌙 Midnight Deal | Rs. 3,100 | 2 Large Pizzas + 1.5L Drink |
| 🎓 Student Deal | Rs. 600 | 1 Small Pizza + Tin Pack Drink |

### 📖 Story Section
- Brand story and values
- Fresh ingredients promise
- Local community focus
- Three key selling points:
  - ✓ Fresh ingredients, prepared daily
  - ✓ Generous portions and honest prices
  - ✓ Fast local delivery and easy pickup

### 📞 Contact Section (Super Simple)
- **Heading**: "YOUR NEXT MEAL IS ONE MESSAGE AWAY"
- **Rider Image**: Custom delivery bike SVG (visible on all devices)
- **Two CTA Buttons**:
  - 📱 Order on WhatsApp (with icon)
  - 📞 Call Now (with icon)
- Clean, minimal design focused on conversion

### 🔗 Footer
- Social media links (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp)
- Auto-updating copyright year
- Location tagline: "Kallar Syedan · Made fresh, served hot."
